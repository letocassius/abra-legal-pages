Privacy Policy

Updated October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hangzhou Kefeng Technology Co., Ltd. ("we") handles information when you use Abra, an AI character social app.

Information we handle

Account data includes your email address, account identifier, and sign-in information received from Apple, Google, or email authentication. Apple or Google may give us a relay email or limited profile information according to your choice.

You may provide profile names, photos, posts, comments, messages, character descriptions, uploaded media, feedback, and reports. We also process the actions needed to operate the app, such as follows, likes, blocks, and notification preferences.

The app and its service providers may process technical information needed for security and delivery, such as device or push-notification identifiers, request metadata, and error information. Abra stores app state and recoverable drafts on your device.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure your account, display and synchronize your content, run social features, generate AI character content and replies, send requested notifications, respond to feedback, and prevent abuse.

AI processing is optional. After your first sign-in, Abra presents Privacy settings so you can review the policy and choose whether to enable AI sharing. Before sending your content for AI processing, Abra requires your explicit permission. Declining leaves browsing and account management available. Messages, comments, posts with AI reactions, and AI generation require permission. You can review or withdraw it in Settings → Privacy settings. We record the disclosure version, your choice and its timestamp for your account.

AI providers, content and processing locations

Volcengine (ByteDance), through the Volcano Ark service, provides Seed for character conversations, text and profile planning, and image understanding, and Seedream for image generation. These requests use the mainland China service. Relevant messages and conversation history, character descriptions, post and comment text, prompts, and images selected for AI features may be processed there, including content that identifies you.

Google provides Gemini for avatar image generation and as an operational fallback for text, image understanding and image generation. A fallback may send the same relevant content to Google. Processing may take place outside the United States. We do not use MiniMax in the active app workflow.

Abra sends the content needed to provide the requested feature, including the relevant history and character context used to keep replies consistent. Sign-in passwords, authentication tokens and email addresses are not included as account fields in AI prompts. Text you write or images you choose may themselves include personal information. Avoid submitting sensitive information or another person’s information without permission.

Volcengine’s published Ark terms say it does not use customer data to train or optimize models without separate consent, while permitting certain compliance, security and customer-requested troubleshooting processing. This is not a guarantee of zero retention. Its platform terms are available at https://console.volcengine.com/ark/region:cn-beijing/docs/volcengine-ark-platform-terms.

Google’s data handling depends on the API service and billing terms. Under its paid Gemini API terms, prompts and responses are not used to improve Google products, but limited abuse-monitoring logging may still occur. Unpaid service terms permit product-improvement use and human review; private user content must not be routed through an unpaid service for this release. See https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms. Provider retention exceptions and legal obligations may apply.

Withdrawing permission cancels queued AI work and prevents new provider requests for your account. An already transmitted request may finish. Withdrawal does not delete earlier posts, messages or provider security records; use account deletion or contact us to request deletion. You may still view existing content, which may have been generated before withdrawal.

Sharing and visibility

Your profile, posts, and other content may be visible to other users of the service according to the feature's visibility settings. Direct messages are intended for their participants, subject to necessary service operation and safety review.

We use service providers for authentication, data storage, media storage, notifications, and AI processing. They receive information needed to perform those services. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

We retain account information and content while your account is active and as needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the service. Copies may remain temporarily in backups and security records according to operational and legal requirements.

You can delete your account in Account security. This requests deletion of your sign-in account and associated app data and uploaded media. You may also email us to request access, correction, or deletion. Some information may be retained where law requires or permits it.

Your choices and security

You can edit your profile, manage notification permissions through your device, block characters, and delete your account in the app. You can manage Apple or Google permissions with those providers.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No service can promise absolute security. Service providers may process information in countries other than yours, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

Children and changes

Abra and its AI features are intended only for adults age 18 and older. Do not create an account or use AI features if you are under 18. Contact us if you believe a minor has provided information so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the updated date and provide additional notice where required.
