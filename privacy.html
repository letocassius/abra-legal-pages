Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-10-27

This Privacy Policy explains how Hangzhou Kefeng Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares and retains information when you use Abra. Abra is a personal social world of fictional AI characters. You are interacting with AI, not with the real people those characters might resemble.

Information we collect

Account information includes your email address, account identifier, username, profile names and the limited information supplied by your chosen email, Sign in with Apple or Google sign-in service. Apple may provide a private relay email address. Sign-in with Google is separate from Google Gemini; Gemini is not an active AI provider in Abra.

User content includes profile photos, posts, comments, direct and group messages, character descriptions, generation prompts, selected reference photos, uploaded media, feedback and reports. Photos and text can contain information about you or other people. Abra does not need access to your entire photo library to process the photos you choose.

App activity includes searches, viewed posts, content delivery and read receipts, likes, follows, saves, blocks, reports and notification preferences. We use account and profile identifiers to keep your personas, content and recommendations associated with the correct account.

Technical information includes installation identifiers, push notification tokens, request and network metadata, and operational error and security records. The app also keeps local caches, recoverable drafts and pending operations on your device.

Why we use information

We use information to authenticate and secure your account; store, display and synchronize content; operate messages and social features; rank and personalize your feed and search; generate AI replies, character content and images when you permit it; deliver notifications you enable; handle reports and support requests; and prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal information or use your content for third-party advertising. Abra does not use an advertising identifier or an advertising tracking SDK in its current configuration. Personalized recommendations inside Abra are separate from tracking your activity across other companies’ apps and websites.

Your choice before AI sharing

Abra is an AI-powered social service. AI processing is required to use its social features and requires your separate, affirmative agreement after sign-in. Merely signing in or reading this policy does not enable AI processing. If you decline, Abra returns you to sign-in and does not allow access to the social app.

Before sending account content for AI processing, Abra checks the current permission recorded for that account. Relevant messages and conversation history, character details, posts, comments, prompts and selected photos may be sent to BytePlus for the purposes described below. Your permission covers the ongoing AI replies and reactions enabled by the features you use, not only the first generation.

You can read this policy, recover access and manage or delete your account without agreeing to AI processing. To withdraw your agreement, contact abraapp2026@gmail.com from your account email; we may verify your identity before recording the withdrawal. Once recorded, withdrawal stops new AI requests and prevents further use of the social app unless you agree again. Requests already sent may finish. You can sign out while a request is being handled. We retain the disclosure version, your choice and its timestamp. A material change to the AI recipient, data categories or purpose requires a new choice before further processing; existing consent is not silently upgraded.

BytePlus AI processing

BytePlus Pte. Ltd., a ByteDance company registered in Singapore, processes content for Abra’s AI features. Seed generates character replies, text, captions and plans and understands selected images; Seedream generates avatars and post images. We use BytePlus ModelArk’s Southeast Asia endpoint. The country of a service endpoint does not guarantee where all processing, support access or retention takes place.

For conversations, we send the message, relevant conversation history and character context needed to reply. For image features, we send the creative prompt and selected appearance-reference photos; generated images may be sent back for image understanding and quality checks. Post planning and reactions can use relevant posts, comments and character details. This content can identify you or someone shown in a photo. Sign-in passwords, access tokens and your account email are not intentionally added as account fields in AI prompts.

BytePlus is the only active AI recipient. We do not send AI requests to Volcengine, Google Gemini or MiniMax, including as a fallback. A BytePlus failure is returned without switching your content to another AI supplier.

We use these services to deliver AI features, not to sell your content or use it for advertising. Abra does not train or fine-tune models with your content as part of these features. BytePlus processes data under the service and data-processing terms applicable to our account, including its security and legal obligations. We do not promise zero retention or a fixed supplier deletion deadline. You can read BytePlus’s Privacy Policy at https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/legal/PP . Contact us for questions or requests about content processed on Abra’s behalf.

Turning off AI sharing prevents new provider requests for your account and cancels pending account AI work. A request already transmitted may finish. Withdrawal does not erase existing messages, posts, cached content or records already held by a provider. To request deletion, use account deletion or contact us; we will handle provider-related requests under the applicable contractual and legal procedures.

Other service providers

Supabase provides our account authentication, database, media storage and backend processing. It receives account information, stored content and operational records needed to run those services. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google sign-in, that sign-in provider processes the information needed to authenticate you. Google sign-in does not enable Google Gemini processing.

If you enable notifications, Apple Push Notification service receives the device push token and notification payload, which can include a title, preview text and navigation identifiers. Your device may display these on the lock screen according to your notification settings. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings and manage available preferences in Abra.

We engage providers to perform their services subject to applicable privacy and security obligations. We may also share information with authorized support or safety personnel, professional advisers or authorities when needed to respond to your request, comply with law, protect rights or investigate abuse. We remain responsible for assessing the providers used by Abra; a link to a provider policy does not replace our obligations to you.

Visibility and AI conversations

Abra distinguishes shared system-character content, content shown in your personal social world, and private or account-scoped content. The audience controls available for a feature determine where the app shows it. Choosing a private audience does not prevent necessary backend processing or the AI processing you separately allow.

Direct and group conversations involve fictional AI characters. They are stored and processed to provide the service and are not end-to-end encrypted conversations with real human correspondents. Necessary support, safety and legal access may apply.

Uploaded or generated images may be delivered through publicly reachable media URLs. Anyone who obtains one of those URLs may be able to view or copy the image even if its containing post is not shown to them in the app. Do not upload sensitive media. Removing an item from view cannot recall copies already saved or shared by someone else.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information, messages, posts, media and social state while needed to provide your account and the features you use. Drafts and pending generation work are kept as needed to let you resume or recover those actions. Consent records are kept to document your choices. Support, report, security and operational records are retained for the time reasonably necessary to address the issue, prevent abuse, resolve disputes or satisfy legal requirements.

To delete your account, open Settings → Account security → Delete account and confirm the request. Abra cleans up account-scoped uploaded and generated media, including profile, post and message images, then revokes sign-in sessions and deletes the sign-in account and associated app records. Shared fictional system-character media maintained for the service is not owned by an individual account and is not deleted with that account. If a cleanup or deletion step fails, Abra reports that the request did not complete; some media may already have been removed and the operation can be retried. Contact us if you cannot complete deletion or account media remains accessible.

A deletion request does not recall copies already downloaded or remove data immediately from every cache, backup, provider security record or legally required record. Backups and records kept for security, disputes or legal obligations remain only for their applicable retention purpose. We assist with provider-related requests under the applicable contractual and legal procedures. We do not promise a specific number of retention days that has not been established for the relevant service.

Your rights and controls

You can edit available profile information, request withdrawal of AI permission, manage notifications, block or report a character, and request account deletion in the app. For access, correction, export, deletion, restriction or an objection to processing, email abraapp2026@gmail.com. Available rights and response deadlines depend on applicable law. We may verify your identity before responding and will explain any lawful limit on a request.

System permission to access a selected photo or send notifications is separate from permission to share content with BytePlus. Withdrawing one permission does not automatically delete information already stored. You can also manage Apple or Google sign-in permissions with those providers.

Security and international processing

We use access controls, authentication and other technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service. No service can guarantee absolute security. Do not include private secrets or sensitive information in a prompt, message or image.

The operator is Hangzhou Kefeng Technology Co., Ltd. in China. Our infrastructure and service providers, including BytePlus, may process information outside your country, including outside the United States. Applicable contractual and legal safeguards govern international processing; an endpoint location does not establish every processing or support location.

Adults only and policy changes

Abra is intended for adults age 18 and older. Do not create an account or use AI features if you are under 18. The fictional human characters generated for Abra must be adults age 21 or older. Contact us if you believe a minor has supplied personal information so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We will update the date of this policy when it changes and provide additional notice or seek a new choice where required. Acceptance of the Terms of Service does not substitute for the separate permission required to share your content for AI processing.
